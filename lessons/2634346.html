🏠 Design Your Dream Room: Step 1 - Introduction and Research 🏠

🌟 Intro To Project 🌟

Begin by watching the introduction video. Jot down your initial ideas and discuss them with a partner.

🔍 Step 1: Research and Brainstorm 🔍

Use a digital notebook or paper to brainstorm items for your dream room. Sketch your ideas and search online for reference images. Keep in mind the room type (Bedroom, Entertainment Room, Backyard, etc.) and what you would put in it.

  • Think about furniture, decorations, and functionality.
  • Consider the measurements of your items to keep everything proportional in your design.

Create a digital or paper “style board” to organize your ideas and images.

Submit your style board with reference images and item dimensions on Google Slides or another platform. Share and submit the link in this Canvas module.

📚 Materials Needed 📚

  • Computer with internet access
  • Digital notebook or paper sketchbook
  • Pencil or pen

🎯 Learning Outcomes 🎯

  • Develop an organized system for documenting design ideas.
  • Create models based on real-world measurements and proportions.
