Final Export (GIF) & Turn-In

Export a clean looping GIF from Piskel, confirm it works, then submit it with short reflections.

Export from Piskel

  1. File → Export → Animated GIF.
  2. Set 12 fps and check Repeat.
  3. Use Scale 2–3× if canvas is tiny.
  4. Click Download and save.
  5. Play the GIF once to check smooth looping.
File rules
  • Filename: Lastname_Firstname_Piskel.gif
Quality check
  • Loop closes smoothly
  • Logo/emblem visible
  • Motion clear at 12 fps

Turn-In

  1. Upload your .gif to the Final GIF Submission in Canvas.
  2. Answer the 2 reflection questions below.
  3. Submit and check the preview plays.
Filename format: Lastname_Firstname_Piskel.gif

Reflection Questions

  • What is one thing you learned about the design process through this activity?
    Sentence stem: "One thing I learned about design is…"
  • What was the most challenging step of your process, and how did you handle it?
    Sentence stem: "The most challenging part was… I handled it by…"
