Vision Board

Plan the ideas first. Then choose the words and images that make those ideas visible.

Objective: Use words, images, color, and layout to connect who you are now to one direction you want to explore.
Demonstration of learning: Create a board with three words, three images, one present-to-future connection, and a short explanation of one choice.

Open the Vision Board video in a new tab

Plan before you collect images

  1. Use the brainstorm sheet to name interests, strengths, values, a proud moment, a learning goal, and one skill you want to grow.
  2. Choose three words and three images that carry those ideas. Do not collect images merely because they look good together.
  3. Build on paper, poster board, Google Slides, or Canva, using the format your teacher approves.
  4. Write the reflection: One choice that matters on my board is _____ because _____.

Download brainstorm PDFOpen editable DOCX

Privacy boundary: Leave out contact information, passwords, exact addresses, and anything you do not want displayed. A meaningful board does not require a public version of your whole life.

Success check

  • The board includes text and images.
  • The layout shows what matters most.
  • At least one choice connects who you are now to where you want to go.
  • You can explain why you chose one image, word, or color.
