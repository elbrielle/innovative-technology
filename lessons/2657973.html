Pitching Lesson 1 — Personal Logo and Brand

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: personal visual identity

Student objective: Students will design a personal logo with their name, one meaningful icon, and no more than three colors.

Essential TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(12)(H) — select and use productivity tools found in spread sheet, word processing, and publication applications to create digital artifacts, including reports, graphs, and charts, with increasing complexity.
Supporting TEKS:

None claimed for this preparation or operational item. The student product does not independently demonstrate a TEKS expectation.

Demonstration of learning: Completed Google Docs worksheet plus Adobe Express share link. This is the current 5-point minor assignment.

Scope: Practiced

Before class

  • Test Adobe Express through ClassLink and the Google Docs worksheet hand-back path.
  • Open a few logo examples and prepare a personal-icon brainstorm/word bank.
  • Have a paper sketch option ready before students search the asset library.

Five-part lesson flow

  1. Launch: Identify the name, icon, and color choices in an earlier career or identity example.
  2. Model: Sketch then create a simple name, meaningful icon, and three-color logo in Adobe Express.
  3. Plan: Students select an icon tied to themselves and rough-sketch before opening assets.
  4. Create and conference: Students build while you check relevance and stop sticker-library drift.
  5. Evidence: Students paste the share link in the worksheet, submit, and peer-check name/icon/color limits.

Check before students leave

The teacher can open the submitted share link, and the logo meets the name, meaning, and three-color requirements.

Support without lowering the thinking

Keep a personal-trait word bank and the stem ‘My icon represents ____ because ____.’ Allow sketching, shapes, labels, or speech-to-text; do not grade drawing skill.
