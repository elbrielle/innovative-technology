THINGLINK | FACILITATOR GUIDE

Interactive Places and Branching Choices

One tool, two clear ideas: tags guide an audience through a place, and choices change the path through a story.

The two-week route

Week 4: Make a place interactive

Day 1 analyze and plan. Day 2 build the 2D map. Day 3 compare flat and 360 media. Days 4 and 5 build, test, and revise a 360 guide.

Week 5: Choices change the route

Day 1 Two-Door practice. Day 2 story map. Days 3 and 4 build. Day 5 test every path, revise, and publish.

Before students sign in

  1. Create a ThingLink group for this class. Share the group invite code or use the district single sign-on route. Do not ask students to make unmanaged personal accounts.
  2. Test the student sign-in on a student device. Keep every scene for this unit in the same student account.
  3. Confirm that your ThingLink license can share Scenario Builder projects. K-12 sharing requires an Enhanced or Staff Training license. Essential and Individual Teacher accounts cannot share scenarios. If sharing is unavailable, use the linked-scene fallback in the Branching Narratives guide.
  4. Choose the 360 media route: ThingLink library, teacher-provided 360 JPEGs, or small groups using a charged 360 camera. Do not promise a camera route until file transfer has been tested.

Current platform checks

  • 360 image: JPEG, 2:1 aspect ratio, and no larger than 8192 by 4096 pixels.
  • Accessible tags: descriptive titles, meaningful alt text, captions or transcripts for media, high-contrast icons, and a logical tag sequence.
  • Sharing: test the exact audience link in a signed-out or Student View window before students submit.
  • Edpuzzle: optional teacher-connected route only. The student page defaults to the original YouTube video.
