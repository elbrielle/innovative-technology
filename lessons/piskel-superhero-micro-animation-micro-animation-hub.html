Define • Brand Promise + Plot Diagram

Brand Promise refresher: clear value a brand consistently delivers.
Corporate examples: Nike “Inspiration & innovation for every athlete.” | Google “Organize the world’s information.”
Hero translation: “I stand up for the quiet kid.” “I protect our neighborhood from waste.” “I restore power when lights go out.”
BAM!
  1. Plot Diagram — Exposition, Rising Action, Climax, Falling Action, Resolution for one tight moment.
  2. Constraints — 10 seconds • 12 fps • ~120 frames • 1-shot loop (or 2 simple cuts).
  3. Deliverables — Post your brand promise to Padlet; upload your Plot Diagram.

💡 Power-Up Tips: Hotkeys & Loop Tricks
  • Hotkeys: Ctrl/⌘+Z undo • Ctrl/⌘+D duplicate • hold Shift to select.
  • Onion skin: keep on while roughing; toggle off to check stutter.
  • Loop hack: animate to the apex, then duplicate frames in reverse.
  • Specs: 128×128 or 192×192 • 12 fps • Name: Lastname_Firstname_Hero_Promise.
