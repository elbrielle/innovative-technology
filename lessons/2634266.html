Project Overview

⏱️ 10 min

A brand is the identity of a company—how people view and connect with your products. A logo is a simple picture that visually represents that brand. Today, YOU create a logo that shows YOUR personality. You watched EdPuzzle | The Art of Logo Design first. Now build your own.

📖 New Words:
Brand = The identity that represents a company's values and personality
Logo = A simple picture that visually represents a brand
