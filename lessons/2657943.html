TEACHER ONLY · LESSON GUIDE

3D Modeling Lesson 3 — Ring + Print Constraints

Model the key skill, leave the student task visible, and check the existing submission.

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: 3D print constraints and fit

Objective: Students will evaluate a ring model for wall thickness, scale, support, and fit, then explain what they would check if printing failed.

TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(3)(B) — discuss and implement a design process that includes planning, selecting digital tools to develop, test, and evaluate design limitations, and refining a prototype or model; and
  • §126.19(c)(12)(C) — select and use appropriate platform and tools, including selecting and using software or hardware to transfer data;

Demonstration of learning: Ring STL named LastName-FirstName-ring.stl and the existing 2–3 sentence print-troubleshooting reflection.

Use what is already in Canvas

Before students begin

  • Open the student task in Student View and test the exact sign-in, link, file, or submission path students will use.
  • Prepare only the listed existing materials; do not require a personal account or a new unlisted product.
  • Keep the current student page available as the accessible and absence route.

10–15 minutes: measure and model

Wrap a paper strip around a finger, mark it, and measure in cm. Convert to mm, then divide by 3.14. Model the inner Hole cylinder and an outer cylinder 4 mm wider in both directions. Set height to 4 mm, center with Align, and Group. Check the opening and flat base. Demonstrate selecting the complete custom ring, Export, .STL, Downloads, renaming to LastName-FirstName-ring.stl, and uploading to Canvas. Keep the editable model in Tinkercad. The teacher prepares the STL in a slicer. Keep prints in the teacher queue; do not promise a same-day print.

Lesson flow

  1. 1. Launch: Students will model a ring and diagnose what may fail in physical printing.
  2. Model: follow the checklist above, then have students try the same controls on their own design.
  3. 3. Guide: Complete the first decision or step together; conference before students commit to a direction.
  4. 4. Build: Students work from the assignment while you check their models.
  5. 5. Check + next step: Verify this evidence before students leave: Current ring model/STL and print-readiness reflection.

Scaffolds and teacher decisions

Keep the student lesson's bilingual tabs, word bank, models, and current support route in view while students work. Reduce the number of examples or provide a partially completed planning frame before reducing the evidence. If a student needs an alternate format, preserve the same named evidence and use the current rubric or teacher judgment rather than adding a hidden requirement.

Close the loop

Check that the submitted STL opens and contains the complete custom ring. Check the filename LastName-FirstName-ring.stl and read the existing reflection. Note who needs help with the next modeling skill.

Support during work

Point students to the word bank beside the reflection. Say the tool name, show it, and model the sentence starter aloud. Students may rehearse in Spanish before writing. Keep the same task and submission; these supports are not extra assignments.
