Career Fair · Step 1 · 10 points

Choose One Career to Represent

Compare three possibilities, then commit to one you are willing to investigate honestly.

Objective: Compare three careers using interests, strengths, work preferences, and current career information.
Demonstration of learning: Submit three possibilities and one sentence explaining the career you chose for the project.

Start with a real pool

If your class uses Xello, begin with saved careers, favorite clusters, or Matchmaker results. If not, use CareerOneStop or the BLS Occupation Finder. You are choosing one career to investigate, not making a permanent life decision.

Explore with CareerOneStopBLS Occupation Finder

Compare three

For each career, write:

  • the career name;
  • one reason it may fit you;
  • one thing you already know;
  • one thing you still need to investigate.
Fit check: What kind of tasks hold my attention? What do people ask me for help with? Do I want to work mostly with people, information, tools, or ideas? Could I explain this career to a visitor without pretending I already know everything?

Submit

Type your three careers and finish this sentence:

I am choosing _____ because _____. Before I represent it, I still need to learn _____.
