DAYS 4 AND 5 · COMMUNICATE

Talking Robots: School-of-Fish Challenge

A coordinated robot group needs a sender, a shared channel, a response, and safe spacing.

TOPIC

Infrared communication

OBJECTIVE

Students will program a leader and follower, then coordinate two or more RVRs.

DEMONSTRATION OF LEARNING

Students will submit leader and follower code, test evidence, a coordinated run, and a revision claim.

Learn the message system

Open Talking with RVR classroom walkthrough in a new tab

Open RVR: IR You Following Me?

Build + challenge

  • Build the leader program and broadcast on the agreed channel.
  • Build the follower program and follow the same channels.
  • Send message 7 at the end of the leader route.
  • Make the follower stop following, spin, wait, then follow again.
  • Use at least two RVRs to hold a formation without hand steering.
  • Film one run, revise message timing or spacing, and film the improved run.

Turn in

  • Leader and follower program evidence
  • A video or shareable link showing the coordinated run
  • Completed test log and reflection
  • One sentence explaining which message, timing, or spacing change improved the system
