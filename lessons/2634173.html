Complete the Talking with RVR worksheet to learn more about IR, program your Sphero RVRs, and finally create your own program with your classmates to emulate a school of fish making patterns (biomimicry).

🐟 Biomimicry

Copying a pattern from nature to solve a design problem. A school of fish holds its shape without a leader — every fish just reacts to its neighbours. That is exactly what your robots are doing with IR.
