Daily Learning Contract

Topic: 360 media and virtual-space representation

Objective: Students will analyze how 360 imagery and modeled 3D space represent the same place differently, then select media and tools that support navigation.

TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(3)(B) — discuss and implement a design process that includes planning, selecting digital tools to develop, test, and evaluate design limitations, and refining a prototype or model; and
  • §126.19(c)(11)(A) — combine various file formats for a specific project or audience; and
  • §126.19(c)(12)(C) — select and use appropriate platform and tools, including selecting and using software or hardware to transfer data;

Demonstration of learning: One Delightex link containing the completed 360-photo scene and corresponding navigable 3D scene, plus a short comparison naming one strength of each representation.
