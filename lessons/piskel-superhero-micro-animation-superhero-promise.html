What Is Your Hero’s Promise?

Your task: write a one-sentence brand promise for your superhero and get ready to animate it later in Piskel. Keep it short, powerful, and clear enough that an audience understands your hero’s purpose instantly.

⚡ Goal: 1 sentence 🧭 Clear purpose 🎯 Audience ready
✍️

Write Your Brand Promise

Start with the sentence stem. Make it specific and action-oriented:

“My hero always promises to _____.”

🛡️
Example 1

“My hero always promises to protect the powerless.”

💡
Example 2

“My hero always promises to bring hope to the hopeless.”

Brand promise refresher: it’s the clear value a brand (or hero) delivers every time. Corporate examples → Nike: “Inspiration & innovation for every athlete.” • Google: “Organize the world’s information.”
On your paper or in Canvas, complete the sentence:
“My hero always promises to write your promise here… .”
