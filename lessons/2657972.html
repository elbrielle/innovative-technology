Pitching Lesson 0 — Logo Design Launch

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: Visual communication principles in logo design

Objective: Students will explain how simplicity, color, and symbolism help a logo communicate identity.

TEKS: No independent Grade 8 Technology Applications TEKS claim. This lesson provides operational, preparatory, or cross-curricular evidence.

Demonstration of learning: Completed EdPuzzle responses and one design intention that names a principle the student will apply in the next lesson.

Before class

  • Reconnect and test the EdPuzzle with the class code; turn on captions and preview the questions.
  • Project two recognizable, simple logos and prepare the three-color constraint for tomorrow.
  • Keep the current module guide’s EdPuzzle fallback available if the external tool is unavailable.

Five-part lesson flow

  1. Launch: Show one recognizable logo and ask what it communicates without words.
  2. Frame: Explain why the video comes before making a personal logo and preview tomorrow’s name, icon, and three-color rule.
  3. Watch and respond: Students complete the video questions with captions and rewind available.
  4. Debrief: Connect two video design choices to the personal-logo criteria.
  5. Evidence: Verify EdPuzzle completion and collect one design intention for the next lesson.

Check before students leave

Every student has completed the video or has a documented fallback path and can name one choice they will make in their own logo.

Support without lowering the thinking

Use captioning, replay, and a visual logo comparison. Allow a student to say or draw the design intention rather than requiring a written sentence.
