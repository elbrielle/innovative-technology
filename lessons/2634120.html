Materials + prep

  • EdPuzzle class code tested. Four of the thirteen items are EdPuzzles and they carry the direct instruction for three of the four lessons.
  • Webcams working, and camera permission allowed in the browser, for Lesson 3. Teachable Machine asks for the camera on load; a blocked camera is the failure students will report as “the site is broken.” The lesson offers uploading images as a fallback.
  • Two small objects per student for Lesson 3 — a ball and an apple is the page’s own example. Have a bin of spares; the students who forget are the ones who need the lesson most.
  • Physical class journals. Where these pages say to write in a journal, they mean the notebook on the desk. There is no Canvas item behind it.
  • Lesson 2 projected. The map of The Docks and the three-language pseudocode comparison are worth reading together before students write anything.

Watch-fors

  • The lesson numbers run 1, 2, 3, then 5. There is no Lesson 4. That gap is inherited from the source curriculum, not a missing item — nothing was removed from this module. Say it once so nobody goes hunting.
  • Three items open with a blank Canvas page. The two sustainability items and the Lesson 2 EdPuzzle have no description at all; the launch button is the whole item. Normal, and worth one sentence at the start of the unit.
  • The four EdPuzzles carry two-thirds of the module’s points. Each is a 5-point Minor grade, and each page says “Minor Grade,” so the label now matches the gradebook. With the Lesson 1 activity and the Xello check-in at 5 apiece, 20 of the module’s 30 points sit inside EdPuzzle.
  • Lessons 2 and 3 are the two biggest builds in the unit and both are worth 0 points. The algorithm for The Docks and the trained Teachable Machine model are real artifacts. The points sit in the four EdPuzzles, the Lesson 1 activity and the Xello check-in; nothing rides on the two builds themselves.
  • Teachable Machine comes back twice more. Lesson 3 is the first meeting; Image Classification and Machine Learning Week both build on it. Keep the models students train here — being able to point back at their own first model is worth more than a fresh start later.

Grading

Six items carry points, all Minor grades at 5 each: the four EdPuzzles, Activity: Lesson 1 Bon Voyage, Robot! and the Xello Check-in: Education Experiences — 30 points total, and no Major grade in this module. Everything else, including both built lessons and the two Learn launches, is set to 0. The Lesson 1 activity accepts a website link or a file upload; the Xello check-in is a screenshot upload.
