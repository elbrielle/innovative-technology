EXPLORE CAREER MATCHES

Xello matched you with those careers for a reason. Go find it.

⏱️ ~35 min
🛠️ Xello via ClassLink
💻 Your device
📤 Screenshot of lesson complete
  • English
  • Español

📢 You spent this unit getting a robot home by reading its sensors instead of guessing. Do the same with your own results — stop reading the list of matches and start reading why.

Today's words

career aspects • central aspect • match • likes & dislikes

1

Log in to Xello

Open ClassLink and click the Xello tile. Go through ClassLink, not a Google search — that is the only login that connects to your saved work.

2

Complete the lesson

Open Lessons and start Explore career matches. You investigate the central, secondary, and other aspects of the career match assessment and how they relate to careers. About 15 minutes.

Prerequisites: the Matchmaker quiz from the SW1 check-in and 3 saved careers from the SW2 check-in. Missing either one keeps this locked.

At the end you pick one career that interests you and read its aspects. Pick one you are actually curious about, not the first one on the list.

3

Screenshot the completion screen

When the lesson finishes, Xello marks it complete. Capture that screen.

• Chromebook: Ctrl + Show Windows

• Windows: Windows key + Shift + S

4

Upload the screenshot here

Attach the image to this assignment. Your work has to be readable in the picture — a blurry photo of your screen taken with a phone does not count.

💡 A match is not a prediction

It is a list of aspects you said you liked, checked against what the job actually involves all day. Knowing the aspect is more useful than knowing the job title.

Your matches move every time you retake Matchmaker. That is the system working, not you being indecisive.

💬 Reflect

In the comments of your submission, finish this sentence:

"Xello matched me with ______ and the aspect behind that match is ______."

1

Entra a Xello

2

Completa la lección

Abre Lessons y empieza Explore career matches. Investigas los aspectos centrales, secundarios y otros de la evaluación de coincidencias y cómo se relacionan con las carreras. Como 15 minutos.

Requisitos previos: el examen Matchmaker del check-in de SW1 y 3 carreras guardadas del check-in de SW2. Si te falta cualquiera, esto sigue bloqueado.

Al final escoges una carrera que te interese y lees sus aspectos. Escoge una que de verdad te dé curiosidad, no la primera de la lista.

Sube la captura aquí

💡 Una coincidencia no es una predicción

Es una lista de aspectos que dijiste que te gustaban, comparada con lo que el trabajo realmente implica todo el día. Conocer el aspecto sirve más que conocer el nombre del puesto.

Tus coincidencias cambian cada vez que repites Matchmaker. Así funciona el sistema, no es que seas indeciso.
