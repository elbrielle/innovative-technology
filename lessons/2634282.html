Daily Learning Contract

Topic: Circuit design process

Objective: Students will explain how empathy, ideation, prototyping, testing, feedback, and revision work together to produce a circuit solution for a user.

TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(3)(B) — discuss and implement a design process that includes planning, selecting digital tools to develop, test, and evaluate design limitations, and refining a prototype or model; and

Demonstration of learning: Lesson 1 checkpoint or project plan that identifies the user need and correctly sequences the design stages the team will use.
