District Xello route

  1. Open Xello through ClassLink.
  2. Add one life experience from this course. Answer what you liked, what challenged you, and what you learned.
  3. Set two specific goals and add action steps.
  4. Upload one screenshot showing the experience and one showing the goals.

No Xello access?

Use the Canvas text box. Describe one experience from the circuit lab, what challenged you, what you learned, and two goals with one action step each.

One experience that taught me something was ___. I learned ___. My next goal is ___, and my first action is ___.

Word bank: experience · challenge · evidence · goal · action step · short-term · long-term

Before you submit

  • ✓ One experience with all three reflection prompts
  • ✓ Two goals with action steps
  • ✓ Two screenshots, or the complete Canvas fallback
