📊 Day 2: Plot Diagram Your Story

Before you write your script, plan your story with the 5 parts of a plot diagram.

Instructions:

  • Exposition: Who is the main character? Where are they?
  • Rising Action: What problem or challenge begins to form?
  • Climax: What’s the most intense or exciting moment?
  • Falling Action: How is the conflict starting to get resolved?
  • Resolution: How does the story end?

You can submit your plot diagram in any of these ways:

  • Draw it on paper and upload a picture
  • Use a Google Slides or Docs template
  • Type it directly in the text box

Checkpoint (5 pts):

  • ☐ Includes all 5 plot parts
  • ☐ Conflict is clear and makes sense
  • ☐ Story has a beginning, middle, and end
