Career Fair · Step 4 · 10 points

Design the Booth Poster and Handout

Use one visual system to attract attention and leave visitors with something useful.

Objective: Design a poster and handout that attract a visitor, communicate the same career identity, and make the key information easy to scan.
Demonstration of learning: Submit both pieces in one Canvas submission.

Two pieces, one visual system

Poster: career title, hook, three benefit or impact points, one strong image, and a link or QR code to the site.

Handout: the same title, colors, and type choices, plus the three facts a visitor should remember and your name.

Make a copy of the Google Slides templateWatch the Canva tutorial

Creative constraints

  • The poster is at least 11 × 17 inches or uses the same vertical proportion.
  • The headline reads from six feet away.
  • No block of poster text exceeds 15 words.
  • The handout fits on half a page.
  • Both pieces use the same career title, colors, and type system.
Design test: Put the poster on the floor and step back. If you cannot read the headline, the visitor will not walk closer merely because the research is good.

Submit

Upload photos or exported files for both the poster and the handout. PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, and PPTX are accepted.
