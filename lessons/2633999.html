TEACHER ONLY · COMICS 1

Welcome to Smart Solutions + Comic Cover

Outcome: Students assemble the paper booklet they will use for all five Comics lessons and create a cover that uses title, image, color, size, and placement to give readers clear first clues about the hero.

Before class

  • Download the revised Lesson 1 slide deck. Image credits and teaching notes are in the speaker notes.
  • Make one sample folded comic from three sheets of paper. Number the inside pages 1 through 4.
  • Set out pencils, erasers, and coloring supplies. Decide where comics will be stored between classes.
  • Decide whether students will staple at their tables or bring folded booklets to a supervised stapling station.
  • The student Canvas page includes a student cover collage and the cover-layout guide. The deck uses the collage during analysis and the layout guide on the leave-up work slide.

One-period route

  1. 4 minutes: Superpower bellringer and a concrete Smart Solutions welcome.
  2. 10 minutes: Model stacking and aligning first, then folding and creasing, stapling, and the booklet check.
  3. 8 minutes: Analyze the Black Panther and Ms. Marvel covers using title, image, color, size, and placement as evidence.
  4. 4 minutes: Study the authentic student examples and preview the four cover requirements.
  5. 20 minutes: Students sketch three thumbnails, choose one, and build the cover. Leave the cover-outline work slide projected.
  6. 4 minutes: Partner quick read and exit sentence.

Check before students leave

Each cover needs a readable title, the hero's name, one central hero image, and colors or details that fit the hero. Students should be able to name one trait or idea and point to a visible design choice that communicates it. Drawing skill is not the target.

Support without lowering the thinking

  • Keep the three thumbnail frames visible before students commit to a final design.
  • Allow stick figures, traced shapes, labels, and collage.
  • Keep the trait word bank visible.
  • Use the stem: "My cover suggests my hero is ____ because I used ____."

Handoff to Lesson 2: The cover tells us how the hero looks from the outside. The biography explains why that hero makes particular choices.
