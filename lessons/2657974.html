Pitching Lesson 2 — Personal Brand Video

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: personal narrative and presentation

Student objective: Students will create a seven-or-more-slide personal-brand video using their logo, meaningful images, chosen music, and ten ‘I am…’ statements.

Essential TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(11)(A) — combine various file formats for a specific project or audience; and
Supporting TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(12)(H) — select and use productivity tools found in spread sheet, word processing, and publication applications to create digital artifacts, including reports, graphs, and charts, with increasing complexity.

Demonstration of learning: Google Docs worksheet with a share link to the finished Adobe Express or Canva video. This is the current 100-point major assignment.

Scope: Demonstrated

Before class

  • Provide headphones, the Lesson 1 logo, the ‘I am…’ brainstorm, and checked student examples.
  • Test Adobe Express, Canva, the Google Docs worksheet, and a non-owner share-link opening.
  • State music-use expectations and remind students to replace any default track.

Five-part lesson flow

  1. Launch: View one student example and identify how image, music, and text work together.
  2. Model: Import a logo, make one meaningful slide, replace default music, and publish a shareable result.
  3. Plan: Students sequence ten statements and choose images/music before building.
  4. Create and revise: Students build across two periods while you conference for slide count, meaningful images, and changed music.
  5. Evidence: Test one submitted share link as a viewer, then submit through the worksheet.

Check before students leave

The link opens for the teacher and the video contains seven or more slides, the student’s logo, meaningful images, chosen music, and ten identity statements.

Support without lowering the thinking

Give a slide-planning grid and sentence stems for ‘I am…’ statements. Students may record or select text-supported music choices, but the evidence remains a coherent digital composition.
