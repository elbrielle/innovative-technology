🤖 RVR Robotics · Seeing

Do: Seeing with RVR

There is a colour sensor on the underside of the RVR. Teach the robot to react to whatever it rolls over.

🤖 Sphero RVR
🎨 Colour sensor + LEDs
🏆 5 points
1

Watch the walkthrough

This shows the colour sensor doing its job.

2

Build it

Complete the Seeing with RVR worksheet to practise using the Sphero RVR colour sensors to detect the colour around it and change its LED to match the colour card it is detecting.

🎨 What "seeing" means here

The sensor only reports the colour of the surface underneath the robot. Your program is what decides to do something about it — here, matching the LED to the card it just found.

📤 Turn in: Submit the worksheet through this assignment once the LED matches every colour card you test.
