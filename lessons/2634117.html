■ GAME DESIGN • FACILITATOR’S GUIDE ■

VIDEO GAME DESIGN

Two class periods in MakeCode Arcade: build from a tutorial, then remix it into something nobody else has. Everything you need to run it.

⏱️ 2 class periods🎮 MakeCode Arcade🔒 Microsoft sign-in required🏆 Make: 2 tutorials, then a remix

RUN SHEET

Both lessons are self-contained Canvas assignments with the full lesson inside — hook, word bank, numbered steps, an early-finisher menu, turn-in steps, a reflection stem and an end-of-class checklist, in English and Spanish tabs. Lesson 2 depends on Lesson 1: students remix a game they built the class before.

Module itemWhat happensDeliverable
LESSON 01: SKILLMAPSign-in day and first build. Students open the MakeCode Arcade skillmap, sign in with their school Microsoft account, and finish two tutorial paths off the pink puzzle pieces. 🔒 THE SIGN-IN IS THE LESSON’S SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE

MakeCode Arcade saves to the account, not to the machine. A student who works a full period signed out has nothing on Lesson 2 day and no game to remix. Walk the room during step 1 and confirm every screen shows a signed-in name before anyone starts a tutorial.

Microsoft — not Google, not Clever, using the school account. On shared laptops, Remember Me stays OFF, and tell students not to clear cookies between the two classes.

MATERIALS + PREP

  • One device per student with Chrome, and the skillmap link ready to push out: arcade.makecode.com/--skillmap. Lesson 2 starts from the plain arcade.makecode.com instead, because students open their own saved projects.
  • Every student’s school Microsoft account confirmed working before Lesson 1. Sort password resets ahead of the period, not during it — this is the one prep item that decides whether the lesson works.
  • Nothing to print, nothing to buy, no class code to generate. The skillmap is open to anyone with the link.
  • Screenshot shortcut on the board. Both lessons are turned in as a screenshot plus a written reflection in the submission comments.

WATCH-FORS

  • This module is the short cut of a longer unit, and it is complete as it stands. Two lessons, sized to the scope and sequence. Lesson 2 looks backward at Lesson 1 and nothing points forward to a Lesson 3, so you can run it as a self-contained two-day block without anything feeling unfinished.
  • The Spanish tab of Lesson 1 is not a translation of the English one. The English tab includes a MakeCode walkthrough. The Spanish tab does not include that video; it has a note showing where a Spanish walkthrough can be added. The Spanish tab also carries the Remember Me off step and a cookies tip the English tab lacks. If your class works in English, say the shared-laptop rule out loud.
  • Early finishers have somewhere to go. Both lessons carry a LEVEL UP menu — a third tutorial or a third feature, and sharing game links with a partner. Point students at it rather than letting the room drift; the sharing step is where the class actually sees each other’s work.

GRADING — 100 POINTS

Lesson 2: Remix is the module’s Major grade, at 100 points, and it is the only item here that moves a grade. Lesson 1: Skillmap is 0 points — it is the practice run that gets students into MakeCode Arcade.

Both lessons already tell students what finished looks like. Lesson 1: signed in, two tutorials done, screenshot uploaded, reflection written. Lesson 2: game renamed, two or more features added and tested, screenshot uploaded, both features named in the comments.

Both items and the module are unpublished. Publish as you go.
