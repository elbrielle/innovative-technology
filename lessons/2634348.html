🏠 Dream Room: Determine Scale 🏠

👩‍💻 Start with the 'Room Scale Template' in Tinkercad 👨‍💻

Begin your design journey in Tinkercad by finding the "Room Scale Template." Here's how:

  1. Go to the TinkerCAD activity titled "Design your Dream Room".
  2. Locate the template "Room Scale Template".
  3. Select "Copy and Tinker" to start working on your design.

🌐 Downloading 3D Files for Tinkercad 🌐

Expand your Tinkercad project with cool 3D models! Here's how you can download them:

  1. Visit free3d.com to explore a variety of free 3D files.
  2. Choose a model you like. Remember, it must be in .stl or .obj format.
  3. Ensure the file size is under 25MB to be compatible with Tinkercad.
  4. Click to download. The file will usually download as a zip file.
  5. After downloading, double-click the zip file to open it.
  6. Drag the .stl or .obj file into your Downloads folder.

Now you're ready to import these files into your Tinkercad project and get creative!
