What controls the sequence

The Smart Solutions S&S spreadsheet is the pacing source. The live Canvas modules deliver that plan. Built lessons may add detail, but they should not silently replace the spreadsheet. Piskel Animation is the one intentional ordering addition: it sits between Comics and Graphic Design.

Career pathway thread

The S&S asks teachers to continue the district's eighth-grade Xello lessons and connect projects to career pathways across the year. Keep those check-ins where the pacing map places them. Do not add a generic career paragraph to every lesson.
