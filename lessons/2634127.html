DAY 3 · DIRECT + DEBUG

Write, Trace + Revise an Algorithm

The test is not whether the steps sound smart. The test is whether someone else can follow them.

PAGE 210 POINTS

Topic · objective · success

Topic: Testable algorithms

Objective: Write, trace, and revise ordered robot instructions.

Success: 6 to 10 ordered steps, one IF/THEN decision, one stop or repeat rule, partner feedback, and one revision.

Download Mission Log · Word · Make a Google copyDownload Mission Log · PDF

Open Permission to Come Aboard with Algorithms in a new tab.

Write → trace → revise

  1. Write 6 to 10 specific steps on Page 2.
  2. Add one IF/THEN decision.
  3. Add one stop or repeat condition.
  4. Give the page to a partner. They act as the robot.
  5. Circle the first unclear step.
  6. Revise that step and explain the change.

Word bank

move · scan · sense · record · compare · if · then · repeat · stop

Turn in · 10 points

Upload Page 2 or submit a clear photo. Include the partner trace and revision.

Stem: “I changed step ___ from ___ to ___ because the test showed ___.”
