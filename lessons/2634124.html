DAY 2 · DESIGN

Robot Mission Design Brief

Start with the job and the person, not the coolest-looking robot.

PAGE 15 POINTS

Topic · objective · success

Topic: User-centered robot design

Objective: Design a robot for one ocean-research job and constraint.

Success: User, need, constraint, sensor/input, process, and output are all labeled.

Download Mission Log · WordDownload Mission Log · PDF

Your mission

  1. Choose one job: Water Watch, Reef Scout, Trash Tracker, or Signal Relay.
  2. Name the user and the information or help they need.
  3. Accept the assigned constraint.
  4. Sketch the robot large.
  5. Label the sensor/input, process, output, and constraint.
  6. Use the partner check before submitting.

Open Activity: Bon Voyage, Robot in a new tab.

Turn in · 5 points

Upload Page 1 of your Mission Log or submit a clear photo.

Stem: “My robot helps ___ by ___. It senses ___ and responds by ___.”
