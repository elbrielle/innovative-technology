DAY 1 · DO

Drawing Mission: Plan, Program, Prove

Code leaves a visible trace. Your evidence should show what changed between the first and final run.

TOPIC

Robot drawing

OBJECTIVE

Students will plan, program, test, and revise a recognizable robot drawing.

DEMONSTRATION OF LEARNING

Students will submit a completed mission sheet plus photo or screenshot evidence of the drawing and program.

Mission

  • Choose a simple word or symbol with short lines, turns, and curves.
  • Sketch a robot-friendly plan before building the program.
  • Connect to the correct RVR and record its robot ID.
  • Run the drawing, capture the result, and make one code revision.
  • Run it again and explain what improved.

Mission sheet

Use the editable Word file or print the PDF.

Download Word mission sheet · Make a Google copy Download PDF mission sheet

Turn in

  • Completed mission sheet
  • Photo or screenshot of the final drawing
  • Screenshot of the program
  • One sentence naming the hardest move and the revision that helped
