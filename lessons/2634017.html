Practice GIF: Post in the Discussion

Build one small, readable motion in Piskel. Save the editable source, export a GIF, and post it below.

✏️

Quick Steps

  1. Draw 3 to 5 unique poses for one small motion.
  2. Duplicate or hold poses until the loop has 12 to 24 displayed frames at 12 fps.
  3. Preview the loop. Fix one jump, pause, or unclear pose.
  4. Download Last-First-Practice.piskel and move it to your class storage location.
  5. Export a repeating GIF, post it below, and check that it plays.
