🎯 LEARNING GOAL

✅ Use digital design tools to create bold, eye-catching Pop Art using shapes, patterns, and color

🎨 POP ART FEATURES

Pop Art uses these 3 features:

🌈

Bright Colors

+ strong borders

🔵🔵🔵

Repeating Patterns

dots, lines, images

📺

Mass Media Vibes

comics, magazines, movies

📖 VOCAB: Path = lines you draw by hand; closing them makes a shape • Border = the edge around a shape • Font = style of text
🎥 VIDEO TUTORIAL

✏️ YOUR MISSION

Design a Pop Art T-Shirt!

Your design MUST include:

🔵🔴🟡

Repetitive Background

dots, stripes, or patterns

✍️

Text

a word or your name (fun font!)

⬜

Border

around shape or text

⭐

Extra Shapes

add whatever you want!

💡 Stuck? Try creating a simple Pop Art nametag first!

⭐ LEVEL UP: Make 4 copies of your design and change the color scheme on each one — like Warhol's famous repeating portraits! Submit all 4 as a panel.

🎯 META DE APRENDIZAJE

✅ Usar herramientas de diseño digital para crear Arte Pop llamativo usando formas, patrones y color

🎨 CARACTERÍSTICAS DEL ARTE POP

El Arte Pop usa estas 3 características:

🌈

Colores Brillantes

+ bordes fuertes

🔵🔵🔵

Patrones Repetitivos

puntos, líneas, imágenes

📺

Cultura Popular

cómics, revistas, películas

📖 VOCABULARIO: Path/Trazado = líneas dibujadas a mano; cerrarlas forma una figura • Border/Borde = el contorno de una forma • Font/Fuente = estilo del texto
