Write Your Superhero Promise

Your task: write a new promise for your superhero and name one action the audience can see. Keep the same superhero and logo from your comic. The promise is new for Animation.

⚡ Goal: write a new promise 🧭 Clear purpose 🎯 Audience ready
✍️

Write the Promise + Make It Visible

Write a new promise for your superhero. Use the stem to name what people can count on your superhero to do and when it matters:

People can count on [hero] to _____ when _____.

🛡️
Example 1

“People can count on Signal to guide lost people when phones stop working.”

💡
Example 2

“People can count on Harbor to warn neighbors before floodwater reaches the street.”

Promise refresher: it tells the audience what they can count on your hero to do. The animation will show one moment when the hero follows through. Word bank: protect • guide • repair • warn • connect • rescue
In Canvas, enter your new superhero promise and the visible action:
Promise: People can count on _____ to _____ when _____.
Visible action: The audience will see this when _____.
📣

Exit Check: Connect Promise to Action

Submit the promise and visible action before you start the Plot Diagram.

CHECKLIST
  • ✅ Your new superhero promise
  • ✅ One visible action that proves it
  • ✅ Your name and your hero’s name
Check: same superhero and logo, one new promise, and one visible action that proves it.

The audience will see the promise when _____.

Done early? Open Piskel for Kids and experiment with one small motion while you wait for the next step.
