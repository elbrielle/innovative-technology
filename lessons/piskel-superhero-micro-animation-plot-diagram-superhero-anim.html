Superhero Micro-Animation • Plot Diagram

You are mapping a mini-story that your 10-second Piskel animation will bring to life. Keep it tight, visual, and focused on your hero’s brand promise in action.

What is a Plot Diagram?

It’s the classic story arc—beginning → build → peak → resolve. Use it to plan a clear, visual moment.

1) Exposition ➜ 2) Rising Action ➜ 3) Climax ➜ 4) Falling Action ➜ 5) Resolution
For a 10-second animation: Think in micro-beats. One tight moment with a clear beginning (set), middle (challenge/choice), and end (result) is perfect.

🧭 Exposition (Start)

  • Where and when are we? (setting)
  • Who is in the scene? (hero + any key character)
  • Show a normal moment before the change
Prompt: “We first see my hero where? What looks normal right now?”

📈 Rising Action (Build)

  • Problem appears and tension grows
  • Obstacles test your hero’s brand promise
  • Set up the critical choice
Prompt: “What makes the situation tougher? How is the promise tested?”

🌟 Climax (Turning Point)

  • Hero chooses and acts (the big move)
  • Most intense frame(s) — 1–2 shots max
  • Audience instantly understands the promise
Prompt: “What single action proves your promise beyond doubt?”

🪂 Falling Action (Aftermath)

  • Tension drops; we see the result of the choice
  • Consequences are revealed
Prompt: “Right after the big move, what happens on screen?”

🏁 Resolution (Finish)

  • Story ties off quickly; new normal appears
  • Brand promise is fulfilled or reaffirmed
Prompt: “How do we know the promise made a difference?”

🧩 How to Complete Your Plot

  1. Fill in all five sections (keep each brief).
  2. Choose one moment to animate that proves your brand promise.
  3. Plan for 10 seconds at 12 fps (≈120 frames) — 1–2 simple shots or a loop.
  4. Make notes about motion, timing, camera, and layers (what actually moves?).
  5. Use beginning → middle → end even in a short sequence.
Sentence starters: “We first see…”, “The problem is…”, “My hero chooses to…”, “Right after…”, “In the end…”.

🔦 Example Beats (brand promise → protect the innocent)

Exposition: Hero patrols rooftop; city below looks calm.
Rising: Debris falls toward crowd; danger grows.
Climax: Hero dives and shields a child.
Falling: Crowd recovers; dust settles.
Resolution: Child smiles; people feel safe — promise fulfilled.

✅ Plot Diagram Checklist

  • All five sections completed
  • Brand promise is visible in the beats
  • Timing feels doable for 10 seconds
  • Notes include motion arrows, camera ideas, and layers
  • Simple, readable drawings — clarity over detail

🎯 Quality Targets (what “Meets” looks like)

  • Story beats are easy to understand at a glance
  • Climax shows the promise in one clear action
  • Beginning–middle–end in under 10 seconds
  • Animation plan fits 12 fps (≈120 frames)
Pro tip: If your idea needs lots of text to explain, simplify the action.
⏱️ Work Time: ~20 minutes 🛠️ Support Track: 5–8 sec story okay ⚡ Extension: add a subtle loop
