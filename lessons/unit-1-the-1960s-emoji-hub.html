Digital Product Innovations • Unit Project

CREATE YOUR OWN EMOJI

Define → Ideate → Prototype → Showcase

📋 THE BIG IDEA: You are an emoji designer. Your mission is to research existing emoji, find what's missing, design an original emoji using only shapes in Canva, and pitch it to your classmates — who will act as the Unicode Consortium and vote on whether your emoji gets approved.

🚫 THE RULE: Shapes + Combo App ONLY. No pre-made icons, stickers, templates, or clipart from Canva. If you didn't build it, you can't use it.
