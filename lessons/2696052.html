What makes your computer work?

Today’s goal: I can tell hardware from software and test a simple computer problem.

Use headphones.

1. Watch

Code.org: How Computers Work — Hardware and Software

Look for: hardware, software, CPU, and operating system.

Open the video in YouTube · Video and transcript on Khan Academy

2. Find hardware and software

Hardware: physical parts — your keyboard, screen, headphones, and CPU.

Software: programs and instructions — your browser, apps, games, and operating system.

CPU: the chip that carries out program instructions.

Operating system: software that manages how apps use the hardware. Examples: ChromeOS, Windows, macOS, and Android.

Try it: Point to one piece of hardware. Find one program on your screen.

3. Check the sound

A silent video does not automatically mean broken headphones. Check one thing at a time.

  1. Pause the video. Find its speaker / mute button.
  2. With headphones on, switch the video to mute, then unmute. Play five seconds at a low volume. No headphones? Switch mute on and off while the video stays paused; watch the speaker icon change.
  3. Pause again. Notice what changed.

Still no sound? Check the device volume and headphone connection next. If it still does not work, tell your teacher what you checked and what happened.

Use this pattern: Check one thing → test → notice the result → ask for help if needed.
Example: “The video is still silent after I unmuted it. I’ll check the device volume next.”

Video will not play? Read this, then take the quiz.

Hardware is the physical equipment. Software is the instructions that tell it what to do. The CPU carries out those instructions. The operating system manages how programs use hardware, including sound devices.

For example, your browser is software. Headphones are hardware. The operating system helps the browser use the headphones. A silent video might be muted, the device volume might be low, or the headphones might not be connected.

Try the mute-button check if the player appears. If it does not appear, explain the blocked video and the check you would try in the final quiz response.
