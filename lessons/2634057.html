From Flat Map to 360 Preview

A flat image controls the frame. A 360 image lets the viewer choose where to look. Today you decide when that extra control actually helps.

Flat photo or 360 scene?

Flat photo

The creator chooses the frame. The viewer sees one direction.

360 scene

The viewer looks around the full environment and chooses where to focus.

Connect it to your Welcome Mission

Reopen your flat map. Choose one stop where a first-time visitor would understand more if they could look around the space. That stop becomes your 360 preview.

Sentence stem: A 360 preview would help my visitor ___ at ___ because ___ is outside the flat frame.
