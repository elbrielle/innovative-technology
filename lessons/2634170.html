DAY 2 · CONTROL

Precision Movement Lab

Prediction is not enough. Measure the route, compare results, and revise one variable at a time.

TOPIC

Precision movement

OBJECTIVE

Students will test heading, speed, duration, distance, wait, and continue.

DEMONSTRATION OF LEARNING

Students will submit seven documented tests and one evidence-based precision claim.

Official build steps

Open the Sphero lesson and complete all seven steps. The teacher deck follows the same order.

Open RVR+ Blocks 1: Movement

Seven tests

  • Drive exploration and robot ID
  • Heading, speed, delay, and stop
  • Heading square
  • Roll to distance
  • Wait versus continue
  • Lights and sound with a shape
  • Accuracy extension at a new speed
