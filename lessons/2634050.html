📐 DREAM ROOM: SCALE

Convert real-world measurements for TinkerCAD

  • English
  • Español

📣 TinkerCAD uses millimeters, not feet or inches. Today you'll learn how to convert your real-world measurements so everything fits to scale.

1

Watch: How to Scale Your Room

2

The Conversion Process

3 steps to go from real life to TinkerCAD:

Step A: Feet → Meters

Multiply feet × 0.305

Ex: 31 ft × 0.305 = 9.5 m

Step B: Meters → Millimeters

Multiply meters × 1,000

Ex: 9.5 m × 1,000 = 9,500 mm

Step C: Scale down to 1:100

Divide mm ÷ 100

Ex: 9,500 mm ÷ 100 = 95 mm in TinkerCAD

👀 Full example: Room 31ft × 21ft × 8ft

Dimension × 0.305 × 1,000 ÷ 100 TinkerCAD
31 ft 9.5 m 9,500 mm 95 mm 95 mm
21 ft 6.4 m 6,400 mm 64 mm 64 mm
8 ft 2.4 m 2,400 mm 24 mm 24 mm

📋 Quick Reference

1 ft = 0.305 m   |   1 in = 0.0254 m   |   1 m = 1,000 mm   |   Scale: ÷ 100
