📐 Make your room fit the model

Convert your room and furniture measurements to the same 1:100 scale.

📣 TinkerCAD uses millimeters, not feet or inches. Today you'll learn how to convert your real-world measurements so everything fits to scale.

The Conversion Process

Use 1:100 for every dimension. Keep all digits through Steps A and B; round only the final Tinkercad answer to two decimal places.

Step A: Feet or Inches → Meters

For feet: multiply × 0.305
For inches: multiply × 0.0254
Choose the factor that matches your measurement.

Feet example: 31 ft × 0.305 = 9.455 m
Inches example: 72 in × 0.0254 = 1.8288 m

Step B: Meters → Millimeters

Multiply meters × 1,000

Feet example: 9.455 m × 1,000 = 9,455 mm
Inches example: 1.8288 m × 1,000 = 1,828.8 mm

Step C: Scale down to 1:100

Divide mm ÷ 100

Room: 9,455 mm ÷ 100 = 94.55 mm in Tinkercad
Couch: 1,828.8 mm ÷ 100 = 18.288 mm → 18.29 mm in Tinkercad

👀 Full example: Room 31ft × 21ft × 8ft

Dimension × 0.305 × 1,000 ÷ 100 TinkerCAD
31 ft 9.455 m 9,455 mm 94.55 mm 94.55 mm
21 ft 6.405 m 6,405 mm 64.05 mm 64.05 mm
8 ft 2.44 m 2,440 mm 24.40 mm 24.40 mm

📋 Quick Reference

1 ft = 0.305 m   |   1 in = 0.0254 m   |   1 m = 1,000 mm   |   Scale: ÷ 100

El proceso de conversión

Usa la escala 1:100 para cada medida. Conserva todos los decimales en los pasos A y B; redondea solo el resultado final de Tinkercad a dos decimales.

Paso A: Pies o pulgadas → Metros

Para pies: multiplica × 0.305
Para pulgadas: multiplica × 0.0254
Elige el factor que corresponde a tu medida.

Ejemplo en pies: 31 ft × 0.305 = 9.455 m
Ejemplo en pulgadas: 72 in × 0.0254 = 1.8288 m

Paso B: Metros → Milímetros

Multiplica metros × 1,000

Ejemplo en pies: 9.455 m × 1,000 = 9,455 mm
Ejemplo en pulgadas: 1.8288 m × 1,000 = 1,828.8 mm

Paso C: Escalar a 1:100

Divide mm ÷ 100

Cuarto: 9,455 mm ÷ 100 = 94.55 mm en Tinkercad
Sofá: 1,828.8 mm ÷ 100 = 18.288 mm → 18.29 mm en Tinkercad

👀 Ejemplo: Cuarto 31ft × 21ft × 8ft
