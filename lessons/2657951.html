AR Lesson 3 — Interactive AR Shopping

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: interactive AR coding

Student objective: Students will use CoBlocks to build a three-scene shopping experience in which a customer can click to buy or continue browsing.

Essential TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(1)(E) — develop, compare, and improve algorithms for a specific task to solve a problem;
Supporting TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(4)(C) — transfer current knowledge to the learning of newly encountered technologies.

Demonstration of learning: Lesson 3 Canvas link or file upload showing three item scenes and functioning click behavior. A welcome screen, cart counter, and totals remain optional extensions.

Scope: Practiced

Before class

  • Open a teacher-built click-to-buy example in Delightex/CoBlocks.
  • Prepare a short visible vocabulary bank: scene, click, event, action.
  • Have students identify product images/models before they build.

Five-part lesson flow

  1. Launch: Compare a display with an interaction: what changes after the customer clicks?
  2. Model: Trace one click event from an item to the next scene or action.
  3. Plan: Students sketch three products/scenes and the customer path before coding.
  4. Build and debug: Students build, peer-test, and repair click-to-buy or browse behavior.
  5. Evidence: Students submit and have a partner demonstrate every click route.

Check before students leave

A partner can follow the intended customer path without getting stranded in a scene.

Support without lowering the thinking

Keep an event-to-action sentence stem visible: ‘When the customer clicks ____, the app ____.’ Offer a three-box scene map; do not replace the student’s interaction logic with teacher code.
