WHAT WILL THE LASER DO?

Watch the short video, then read one design like the machine will.

Topic
Cut, score, engrave
Objective
Predict the result of a path or fill
Evidence
Name one path and one engraved area

Open the video on YouTube

Watch for three actions

Cut

The beam travels through the material so a piece can separate.

Score

The beam draws a shallow line on the surface.

Engrave

The beam removes or darkens a filled area on the surface.

Stop and jot

Look at the real examples in the next activity. Identify:

  • one path that should cut all the way through
  • one mark or area that should stay on the surface

Sentence stem: The laser should ______ the ______ because ______.

Word bank: path · fill · outline · cut · score · engrave · surface
