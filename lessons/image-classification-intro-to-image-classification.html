📢 Your phone can unlock with your face. A self-driving car can spot a pedestrian. Today YOU teach a computer to tell a thumbs up from a thumbs down.

Today's words

computer vision • image classification • model

1

Open Teachable Machine

Go to teachablemachine.withgoogle.com → Get Started → Image Project → Standard image model.

2

Name your two classes

Rename "Class 1" to Thumbs Up. Rename "Class 2" to Thumbs Down.

3

Take 30+ photos per class

Click Webcam, hold the button, and move your hand around. More photos = smarter AI.

4

Train your model. Then try to break it.

Click Train Model. Watch the preview. Then test: hold your thumb sideways, switch hands, change your background. Where does it fail?

🎥 Stuck? Watch this 2:24 walkthrough.

If you missed something in class, this video walks you through every step. Headphones on.

🔎 TRY TO BREAK YOUR AI

• Hold your thumb sideways

• Move closer or farther from the camera

• Try with your other hand

• Cover part of your hand

• Use a different background

Remember where it fails. We'll talk about WHY all week.
