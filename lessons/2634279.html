🎨 Day 3: Storyboard Your Scenes

Create a simple storyboard showing how each part of your story will be animated.

Instructions:

  • Sketch or describe 6–12 scenes
  • Write what’s happening in each scene
  • Include character actions, props, and movement

Checkpoint (5 pts):

  • ☐ At least 6 scenes are planned
  • ☐ Follows the script in order
  • ☐ Notes are clear about what happens
