🏛️ Who Decides Which Emoji Exist?

The Unicode Consortium is a group of tech companies (Apple, Google, Microsoft) that votes on which emoji get added to every phone in the world. A new emoji takes 1–2 years to get approved.

So I have to convince them my emoji should exist? 😮

Yep. That's YOUR job in this project. 📝

👍👎 What Makes an Emoji Get Approved?

👍 Gets a YES

It's popular — people already search for it online

It's missing — there's a gap in an emoji family it would fill

It's unique — you can't express it by combining existing emoji

👎 Gets a NO

Too specific — only a tiny group of people would use it

Already exists — a similar emoji is already out there

Brand/logo — no Nike swoosh, no Mickey Mouse, no TikTok logo
