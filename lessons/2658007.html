Stop Motion Day 2 — Plot Diagram

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: Narrative structure for production

Objective: Students will apply narrative structure to organize a coherent sequence from exposition through resolution for production.

TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(3)(B) — discuss and implement a design process that includes planning, selecting digital tools to develop, test, and evaluate design limitations, and refining a prototype or model; and

Demonstration of learning: Submitted five-part plot diagram in an allowed format, with a partner check that correctly identifies the climax and traces the conflict to its resolution.

Before class

  • Prepare a five-part plot visual, a familiar-story model, and paper or Google template options.
  • Post bilingual plot vocabulary and sentence stems where students work.
  • Check the current external-tool submission path if using a template.

Five-part lesson flow

  1. Launch: Retell a familiar story using the five plot parts.
  2. Model: Complete one plot diagram from the prior day’s story plan.
  3. Map: Students draft all five stages.
  4. Partner check: Partners identify the climax and test the conflict sequence.
  5. Evidence: Students submit in one allowed format and flag revisions for the storyboard.

Check before students leave

The submission names all five plot parts and shows a coherent beginning, middle, and end.

Support without lowering the thinking

Use a color-coded plot mountain and offer word/phrase cards. Let students explain the plot orally before recording it; do not accept a storyboard in place of the explicit five-part structure.
