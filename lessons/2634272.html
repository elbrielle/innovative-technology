FACILITATOR’S GUIDE · SW6

Facilitator’s Guide: Closing Week

Two items, one week, and the year gets packaged into a single link.

Instructional Alignment

Topic: Digital portfolio publishing

Student objective: Students will Curate and share a public portfolio of completed work

Essential TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(8)(B) — create and publish a formal digital communication for a global audience using appropriate digital etiquette; and
Supporting TEKS:
  • §126.19(c)(11)(A) — combine various file formats for a specific project or audience; and
  • §126.19(c)(11)(B) — share and seek feedback on files in various formats, including text, raster and vector graphics, video, and audio files.
  • §126.19(c)(12)(H) — select and use productivity tools found in spread sheet, word processing, and publication applications to create digital artifacts, including reports, graphs, and charts, with increasing complexity.

Demonstration of learning: one ePortfolio page and share link.

Alignment status: D

⏱ ~2 class periods🌐 Make: one published ePortfolio page💻 Adobe Express · Canvas survey

The unit at a glance

Module itemWhat happens~Time
Closing Week — run the portfolio first, the survey last
Create an ePortfolioOne published Adobe Express web page holding the year: a title with their name and a meaningful image, a short introduction, a photo or link and a description for every project, and their personal logo from Pitching and Presenting. They submit the published share link.1–2 periods
Student: Post-SurveyThe twin of the Pre-Survey from Week 0. Not for a grade — it exists to measure what changed across the year. Run it in class, in the last minutes of the final period.~10 min

Materials + prep

  • Adobe Express through ClassLink, signed in, tested.
  • Their work, gathered before they start building. The portfolio doc from Week 0, Portfolio Part 2, the Tech for Good Capstone, and their personal logo from Lesson 1: Personal Logo and Brand. A student hunting for files is a student not building a page.
  • The student ePortfolio examples linked in step 1 — open one together before they start.
  • The Post-Survey link confirmed working on a student device, not just yours.

Watch-fors

  • The tutorial video says Adobe Spark. Spark is Adobe Express now. The assignment already warns about it: where the video says Spark, choose Webpage from the Get Started templates. Say it before you press play or you will answer it fifteen times.
  • Submission is a published URL, not a file. Students have to publish the page and paste the share link. An unpublished draft link opens for them and for nobody else — check one submission early in the period and you will catch the pattern before the whole class repeats it.
  • The survey is not graded, and it still needs to be done. Give it real class time at the end rather than assigning it as homework on the last day, or the response rate collapses.

Grading

Create an ePortfolio is the module’s Major grade, at 100 points, submitted as a website URL — that link is what you review. Student: Post-Survey is 0 points, ungraded by design, and says so to students.
