🌈 Vision Board Assignment Submission Guidelines 🌈

Versión en Español aquí The source course does not currently contain the linked Spanish Vision Board page.

🌟 General Objectives 🌟

TEKS Section 127.2 (4)(D): Apply core academic skills to meet personal growth and goal-setting.

Aim: Students will learn the importance of self-reflection and its role in personal, academic, and career goals.

Materials Needed: Laptop/Tablet, Pencil, Paper, art supplies for vision board.

📝 Preliminary Steps 📝

  • Self-Reflection Time: Use the camera on your device to look at yourself and reflect on what you see. This is more than skin-deep—go beyond the physical.
  • The Mirror of Self-Reflection: Complete Part A of your worksheet based on your reflections.

🎨 Vision Board Creation 🎨

Criteria: Create a vision board using art supplies, magazines, scissors, glue, and poster board or cardboard. You may also opt for digital platforms like Google Slides, Vectr, or Canva.

Elements to Include:

  • Images that represent your goals, dreams, and aspirations.
  • Text that resonates with your objectives.
  • Personal touches like drawings or mementos.

🔥 Requirements 🔥

  • 🎨 Create one vision board.
  • ✒️ Must include text.
  • 🖼️ Must include images.
  • 🌟 Add personal touches like drawings or mementos.

🌟 Final Touches 🌟

Make sure your vision board aligns with the essence of your self-reflection and future goals. If you choose a digital platform, make sure to download your vision board as a PDF or image file.

📄 Project Submission 📄

Platform: Submit your completed vision board through Canvas.

File Types: Acceptable file types include PDF, JPG, PNG, or a shared link to your digital vision board.
