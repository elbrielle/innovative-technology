Teacher Run Card: Closing Lesson 2 — Post-Survey

Daily Learning Contract

Topic: year-end reflection and data collection

Student objective: Students will complete the post-survey accurately and independently.

Essential TEKS:

None claimed for this preparation or operational item. The student product does not independently demonstrate a TEKS expectation.

Supporting TEKS:

None claimed for this preparation or operational item. The student product does not independently demonstrate a TEKS expectation.

Demonstration of learning: Completed ungraded Canvas post-survey. It is a course-measurement activity, not an independent TEKS demonstration.

Scope: Operational check-in; no TEKS claim

Before class

  • Test the survey on a student device and reserve protected in-class time.
  • Prepare a brief privacy and purpose statement.
  • Use a completion tracker that does not expose survey responses.

Five-part lesson flow

  1. Launch: Explain why the survey matters and that it is not graded.
  2. Orient: Show where to open it and state the expected completion time.
  3. Respond: Students complete it independently.
  4. Verify: Check completion status, not individual answers.
  5. Close: Thank students and give unfinished respondents a clear completion path before they leave.

Check before students leave

Canvas records a completed survey submission; no response content is publicly discussed or graded.

Support without lowering the thinking

Read directions aloud, provide quiet time, and offer accessibility support for navigation. Preserve privacy: adults may help students access the survey but should not influence their responses.
