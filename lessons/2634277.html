📘 Day 1: Brainstorm & Story Planning

Today you come up with your story idea. Choose your characters, your setting, and your conflict. This is what your script and your animation are built on.

Instructions:

  • Pick a story genre (funny, drama, sci-fi, fantasy, etc.)
  • Create a basic outline of:
    • Main character
    • Setting (where and when?)
    • Conflict (what's the problem?)
    • Resolution (how will it end?)
  • Submit your plan as a paragraph, list, or mind map

Checkpoint (5 pts):

  • ☐ Has a clear main character
  • ☐ Includes setting and conflict
  • ☐ Uploaded on time
