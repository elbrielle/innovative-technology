# Genially and Visme Alternate-Track Research

Research date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: free/basic classroom routes that could preserve interactive-experience learning when a campus does not receive ThingLink or Delightex

## Recommendation

Pilot **Genially first** as the alternate for ThingLink-style interactive images and branching narratives. Keep **Visme as a fallback/choice pilot**, mainly for interactive presentations, infographics, product explainers, and data-rich screens.

Do not describe either product as a free replacement for Delightex AR/VR. The current official feature surfaces support interactive screens, hotspots, popups, internal navigation, branching, multimedia, and games. This research did not find official evidence of native Merge Cube authoring, camera-based AR overlays, Delightex-style 3D scene building, physics, or student coding in either product. That is an evidence boundary, not a claim that a hidden or future feature cannot exist.

## What the existing VILS sequence actually needs

| Current unit | Core learning worth preserving | Tool-specific feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ThingLink Welcome Mission | Design an interactive place for a first-time visitor; choose purposeful tags; test navigation. | ThingLink image/scene tags. |
| ThingLink 360 Guide | Organize a place spatially; combine media; guide an audience through it. | Native 360/panorama viewing. |
| ThingLink Branching Narratives | Map choices, connect paths, test every route, publish and revise. | Scenario Builder/branching. |
| Delightex AR Worlds/Shopping/Game | Prototype an interactive experience; combine media; program behaviors; test and revise. | 3D scenes, Merge Cube, CoBlocks, physics. |
| Delightex VR/360/Novel | Build spatial scenes and a navigable narrative with animation, sound, and scene transitions. | 3D/360 scene authoring and VR viewing. |

The first three rows translate well to Genially if the 360 lesson becomes an **interactive-place guide** rather than a false 360 claim. The Delightex rows require a deliberately different product and evidence model.

## Recommended student workflow to pilot in Genially

1. Teacher creates an Education Workspace and receives district approval before adding students.
2. Students join through the teacher-issued code as Student seats; do not create unmanaged personal accounts.
3. Every project uses a findable name: `Period-Last-First-Project` or a district-approved anonymous alternative.
4. Students start from a tested free template or a teacher-created base.
5. Students plan the screen/scene map before opening the editor.
6. Students build, preview, run a peer navigation test, and revise one documented failure.
7. Teacher reviews the creation inside the workspace and gives feedback there.
8. Student submits a small Canvas evidence packet: project name, planning map, one or two screenshots, peer-test result, and revision note. A public URL is not required.

The pilot must specifically test whether a free workspace remains manageable without paid class/project spaces. Unlimited seats are not the same as a good grading queue.

## Proposed alternate curriculum architecture

### A. ThingLink-equivalent Genially unit

Keep the current instructional arc and rename only what the medium changes:

- **Welcome Mission:** clickable campus/community map or illustrated place with purposeful hotspots.
- **Interactive Place Guide:** a wide photo, map, or designed scene with layered media and directional navigation. Do not label it 360 unless the pilot proves a native, reliable 360 route.
- **Branching Choices:** a two-door challenge, approved story map, multi-path build, route testing, and revision.

Use a shared rubric for purpose/audience, navigation, media choices, branching logic, testing, accessibility, and revision.

### B. Consolidated unlicensed alternative to Delightex

Replace the current approximately 15-period AR + VR sequence with an **8–10 period Interactive Experience Studio**:

1. **Interactive product explorer:** hotspots, product details, audience needs, and navigation testing.
2. **Challenge/escape experience:** rules, feedback, branching, and failure/retry paths.
3. **Branching story capstone:** scene map, multimodal evidence, peer testing, revision, and showcase.

This path preserves design process, multimodal publishing, audience testing, and tool transfer. It does not claim 3D, physics, Merge Cube, VR, or programming evidence. The reclaimed time can support the Tech Apps 8 bridge work documented in the standards gap plan.

## Pilot gates

The alternate should not move into Canvas authoring until all of these pass:

- district approved-app/DPA decision;
- teacher and minor-student account flow;
- no unmanaged public student identity requirement;
- Chromebook creation performance with a realistic media load;
- keyboard navigation, headings, alt text, contrast, and readable mobile/Chromebook playback;
- teacher can find and review every project without student public links;
- project recovery after tab closure or connection loss;
- stable Canvas evidence route;
- only free assets/features are used in the exemplar and templates;
- a teacher can run the unit without reverse-engineering the platform.

## Procurement observations

- ThingLink currently offers a 30-day teacher trial; student accounts are not free, and K–12 licensing is custom. A teacher license alone does not provide student creator accounts.
- Delightex Basic is free but limited to one class, one assignment, two projects, 29 students, basic CoBlocks, and no Pro physics/Merge Cube access. It is not a full no-cost replacement for the current four-build AR unit plus VR sequence.
- Before requesting quotes, collect actual campus/teacher/student counts and distinguish teacher authoring seats from student creator seats. A low headline price that cannot cover class sections is not a usable procurement option.
