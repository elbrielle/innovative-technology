### Lane 1 decision guardrails

- Genially is the leading alternate for interactive images, hotspots, branching, games, and escape-room structures.
- Visme is a secondary pilot, not the default, because its free plan is centered on individual public projects and does not provide the same free teacher-managed student workflow.
- Neither product is currently evidenced as a native replacement for Delightex 3D scenes, Merge Cube, physics, or CoBlocks. Alternate materials must name the different medium honestly.
- Minor student Genially seats cannot publish or share publicly. The expected pilot route is teacher-workspace review plus a small Canvas evidence artifact, not a student public URL.

### Lane 2 placement hypothesis

1. **Flexible recurring strand:** keyboarding mini-lessons and benchmark/growth evidence, with optional full-period launch and remediation lessons; file organization, storage choice, and shortcuts recur beside it.
2. **Intro to CS Days 1–3:** introduce and revise structured pseudocode inside the existing Code.org sequence rather than adding a separate unit.
3. **Video Game Design + RVR:** extend the same pseudocode planning routine to variables, abstraction, collaboration, timeline, literal testing, and iteration; add a short explicit text-based nested-loop bridge after students have block-code experience.
4. **Graphic Design + AI Ethics:** intellectual property, digital footprint, and cyberbullying scenarios.
5. **Graphic Design with the 1960s:** technology history, impact, global trends, and prediction.
6. **SPIKE PRIME:** required Sheets formulas, charts, data analysis, prediction, and publication.
7. **Reclaimed alternate-track time at unlicensed campuses:** targeted iCEV/application-fluency bridges without extending the year.
