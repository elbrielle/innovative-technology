## Adversarial consensus

The reviewers received the combined independent findings and challenged scope and evidence. The crop defect was a material rendering failure, not aesthetic preference. Audience and teacher-note corrections are narrow consistency fixes. Missing-information choices and scoring-rule alternatives already make the worksheet universal; a new rubric, translated 37-event collection, teacher deck, or supplies/budget sequence would expand the owner's request unnecessarily. Preserve the category navigation, real student photographs, concrete event stages, low response burden, and ability to reconsider an event.

Operational Canvas publication states are distinct from instructional audience. The public display fix does not alter source or teacher publication choices. Native Google Docs /copy confirmation and blank-reader sharing evidence are not a student-account submission rehearsal.
