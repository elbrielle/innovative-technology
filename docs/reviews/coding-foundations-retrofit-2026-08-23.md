Existing lessons also receive added work without a revised time budget or scoring route.

Decision: `HOLD`.

### District curriculum

The reviewer found that Day 1 and Day 2 overstate independent evidence for §126.19(c)(2)(A) and (B), the alignment contracts omit Topic and I/P/D status, student pages display statutory language, and the rubric/quiz cannot support the closure claim as written. Ledgers, scope and sequence, and the module audit are stale.

Decision: `HOLD`.

### Student experience

The reviewer found contradictory directions, high executive-function load, incomplete multilingual access, ambiguous Passport routing, limited Day 2 agency, and teacher/curriculum language on student surfaces. The one-line debugging success, no-account route, and block-to-text-to-robot arc are strengths.

Decision: `HOLD`.

### Design and language

The reviewer found Passport rendering/Spanish defects, repetitive card-grid slide composition, small or inaccurate screenshot emphasis, curriculum-language leakage, and no end-to-end projected route. Strong contrast, clear task verbs, and the reusable Passport idea should be preserved.

Decision: `HOLD`.

## Adversarial consensus

All four lenses agreed that the PR must remain draft. The challenge round narrowed two overreaches:

- a wholesale decorative redesign is not required; instructional variety, accuracy, legibility, and authentic evidence are;
- exact Passport numbering is a design choice; the gate is unambiguous evidence space for Game, Trace/Repair, Create/Improve, and RVR.

The district reviewer tightened Day 2 authorship: students must meaningfully construct/use string, number, and Boolean variables with operations and complete both loop structures. A scaffold may supply MakeCode APIs, sprite data, and setup, but not the target evidence.
