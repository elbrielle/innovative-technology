## Adversarial consensus

The strongest challenge is whether the deck now has too many tool slides for a live-teach lesson. Ruling: the owner explicitly asked for the font, font-change, element, and color teaching to exist for teachers who do not demonstrate live, and the standard permits showing the few skills students will watch. The deck still routes to one live demonstration (slide 12) and does not turn the demo into click-by-click slides; each tool slide carries one concept with real evidence.

The second challenge is the color-wheel model. Ruling: the deck uses the traditional red-yellow-blue classroom wheel, states it in the notes, and does not claim it as the printer's model. The A/B posters are original editable diagrams, labeled as such in the notes.

Nothing in this revision rewrites the assignment or adds an assessment. The filename convention is a consistency fix that follows Lesson 2.
