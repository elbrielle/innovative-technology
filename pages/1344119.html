Structural Engineering

Your team builds a lightweight structure and tests how much load it can support.

Building the first structurePreparing drawings and verificationCompleting load testing

What you make or do

  • Build a structure that meets the current design brief's material, size, and loading requirements.
  • Prepare a documentation portfolio containing the advisor-verified team form, a three-view drawing, and a cut-parts list. Bring the structure and documentation in the required plastic storage box.
  • If your team advances, design and build a second structure at the conference in response to a variation of the problem.

This year’s challenge

For 2027, build a balsa structure that transfers a load from the centered test block to the tops of the supports across a nine-inch span. The brief permits at most fourteen linear feet of one-eighth-inch-square balsa and prohibits a substructure. Use the full brief and diagrams for all dimensions and joint restrictions.

How the competition works

Before the event

Complete the pre-built structure and documentation during the current school year, obtain the advisor's verification, and check the current annual check-in requirements.

First round

Submit the pre-built structure and portfolio. The structure is checked and destructively tested; its design efficiency determines advancement.

If you advance

Use a three-hour design-and-construction period to make an on-site structure, which is also destructively tested. Judges also assess teamwork and the required documentation.

Know before you choose: Both competition structures are tested until failure. Bring the required verification and drawings, and wear approved safety eyewear for the on-site build.

How your work is scored

Judges evaluate compliance and structural efficiency, which compares supported load with the structure's own mass. Semifinal scoring also includes the on-site structure, team participation, drawing and parts list, and portfolio.
