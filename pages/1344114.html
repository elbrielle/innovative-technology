Team of 2–4 · Model or prototype, display, and possible sales pitch

Inventions and Innovations

Your team develops an invention or improves an existing idea to meet a real need.

Identifying a needMaking a model and displayPreparing a sales pitch

What you make or do

  • Create a model or prototype using mostly recycled materials. It may show how your idea would work or be a working model.
  • Make a static display that explains the need, your brainstorming, the recycled materials, drawings, and how you built the model.
  • Prepare a sales pitch explaining why someone should invest in your idea. Teams that advance present it to the judges.

This year’s challenge

There is no separate annual theme listed. Your team identifies a need and develops an invention or innovation that uses mostly recycled materials.

How the competition works

Before the event

Research a need, develop the model or prototype and display, and prepare the sales pitch before the conference.

First round

Check in the display and model together. Judges review the display first, then evaluate the models from the highest-scoring displays to choose semifinalists.

If you advance

Give a seven-minute sales pitch and answer questions for two minutes. Your team has one minute to set up.

Know before you choose: The display and model together must fit within 15 inches deep, 30 inches wide, and 48 inches high. Both are required at check-in.

How your work is scored

Judges score the display's explanation of the need and solution, the model or prototype, and the sales pitch and interview for teams that advance.
