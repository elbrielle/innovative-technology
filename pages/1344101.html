Audio Podcasting

You will create an original podcast that reports news from the future.

You record a podcast.You document your process.You create new audio if you advance.

What you make or do

  • Record an original audio podcast that is more than one minute and less than five minutes long.
  • Create square podcast cover art and a PDF portfolio documenting your audio timeline, tools, sources, work log, self-evaluation, and copyright checklist.

This year’s challenge

For Breaking News from the Future, report on a fictional future event, discovery, invention, or crisis as though it is happening live.

How the competition works

Before the event

Plan, record, and edit your podcast. Submit an accessible link to the audio and your portfolio PDF by the national pre-conference deadline.

First round

Judges review the podcast first. The highest-ranking entries then have their portfolios evaluated to select semifinalists.

If you advance

Create additional podcast audio for a new challenge revealed at the conference. You have 36 hours and must submit the audio link and copyright checklist PDF.

Know before you choose: The cover art is a required part of the entry. Your team also brings its own recording and editing equipment for the conference challenge.

How your work is scored

Judges assess the podcast's creativity, organization, style, and listener appeal, along with the portfolio and cover art. The conference podcast is scored separately.
