Mass Production

Your team develops a repeatable production process and uses it to make matching products.

Designing a repeatable productMaking matching copiesDocumenting the production process

What you make or do

  • Manufacture at least three identical copies of the product, including the prototype. Submit one full-size prototype at the conference.
  • Create a portfolio with product instructions, design work, drawings, materials and tools lists, a production plan, a work log, and photos showing the matching products and production process.
  • Prepare to explain the product and production process in a presentation and interview if your team advances.

This year’s challenge

The 2027 product theme is an automata box. Your team must show that it can produce matching copies of its design.

How the competition works

Before the event

Design the product, test and improve the production process, make the matching copies, and document the work before the conference.

First round

Submit one prototype and the documentation portfolio. Judges score prototypes first, then evaluate the portfolios from the highest-scoring prototypes to select semifinalists.

If you advance

Up to three team representatives give a seven-minute presentation and answer questions for two minutes, with one minute for setup.

Know before you choose: You make at least three matching products, but only one prototype is submitted. The portfolio must include photographic proof of the copies and a photo timeline of the team's work.

How your work is scored

Judges evaluate the prototype's quality and function, the documentation of design and production, and the presentation and interview for teams that advance.
