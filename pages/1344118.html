Solar Racer

Your team builds a solar-powered model car and documents how it performs.

Building and testing the carDocumenting solar-only runsRacing and explaining the design

What you make or do

  • Build a model car that runs on solar power and also supports the specified battery setup for indoor conference races.
  • Create a showcase display with the car's name, engineering design process, drawings, components list, and a run log with at least five runs.
  • Record three videos showing the car traveling twenty meters using solar power alone, without batteries in place. Provide an accessible QR-code link to the videos on the display.

This year’s challenge

For 2027, conference racing uses a model solar track placed on top of carpet. The guide requires the conference races to run indoors on two supplied AA batteries, while your videos demonstrate solar-only operation.

How the competition works

Before the event

Build and test the car, record the three solar-power videos, and complete the display and required records before the conference.

First round

After inspection, qualifying cars receive time trials. The fastest entries move to display/model judging and head-to-head races within the preliminary round; those combined results determine semifinalists.

If you advance

At least two team members take part in a five-minute interview about solar power and how the car was made.

Know before you choose: This entry includes a display and three solar-only videos as well as a car. The current update says no official car kit or parts are specified; read it with the guide's component limits.

How your work is scored

Time trials decide which entries continue to display/model judging and head-to-head races. Display/model and race scores select semifinalists, whose interview score also contributes to the final result.
