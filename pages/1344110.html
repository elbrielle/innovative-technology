CAD Foundations

You will use design software to draw an engineering part accurately.

You receive a drawing problem.You create the CAD drawing.You submit a PDF.

What you make or do

  • Create two-dimensional engineering drawings for a problem supplied at the conference.
  • Save your completed drawing as a PDF on a labeled USB flash drive.

This year’s challenge

No separate annual theme is posted. You receive the actual design problem at the conference.

How the competition works

Before the event

Practice interpreting technical drawings and using your CAD software. Bring your computer, software, blank USB drive, and any permitted printed references; you do not bring a finished competition drawing.

First round

After setup, work independently for two hours on the supplied drawing problem. Judges evaluate your work and may ask you questions.

How the event finishes

There is no separate semifinal challenge listed. Allow an additional hour for the final evaluation process.

Know before you choose: You must be able to export the drawing to PDF yourself. National TSA does not provide internet access for your CAD software.

How your work is scored

Judges assess the correct views, line types, dimensions, title block, and use of CAD tools. Completion time breaks ties.
