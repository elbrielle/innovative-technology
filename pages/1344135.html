Tech Bowl

Put your technology knowledge to work.

Three testsOne team scoreHead-to-head questions

What you make or do

  • Study TSA and technology concepts.
  • Answer questions on your own first; compete with your teammates if you advance.

This year’s challenge

No separate annual theme is posted. This event focuses on TSA and technology knowledge.

How the competition works

Before the event

Study TSA and technology concepts and prepare to take the individual knowledge test with your teammates.

First round

All three teammates take a one-hour test individually. Your team ranking uses the average of your scores.

If you advance

Compete head-to-head against another team in a question-and-answer round.

Know before you choose: The same three members must compete in both rounds. This is a knowledge event; there is no project portfolio to create.

How your work is scored

Your average test score determines whether you advance. The team question rounds use points for correct answers and deductions for some incorrect answers.
