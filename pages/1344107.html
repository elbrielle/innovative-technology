Video Game Design

Your team creates a playable online game and explains the decisions behind its design.

Developing three playable levelsDocumenting and demonstrating the gameExplaining the design to judges

What you make or do

  • Create an interactive online game on a subject your team chooses. Judges must be able to play at least three levels, and gameplay must last longer than three minutes.
  • Create a portfolio PDF with the game's purpose, audience and market research, instructions and controls, a sketched storyboard, copyright documentation, and a work log.
  • Include a link to a video of up to two minutes showing a student demonstrating at least three levels with voiceover instructions.

This year’s challenge

There is no separate annual theme listed. Your team chooses the game's subject, and the game must meet the event's requirements for content suitable for everyone, including no weapons or violence.

How the competition works

Before the event

Build and test the online game, record the demonstration video, and submit the game URL and portfolio PDF by the official pre-conference deadline.

First round

Judges play the game's first three levels and review the portfolio, including the demonstration video, to choose semifinalists.

If you advance

Give a presentation of up to five minutes and answer judges' questions for up to five minutes.

Know before you choose: Judges must be able to play the game online without a software download, executable file, or request for access. Keep it playable from the submission deadline through the end of the conference.

How your work is scored

Judges evaluate the game's creativity, technical skill, flow, mechanics, and appeal, plus its documentation and demonstration video. Semifinalists also receive a presentation/interview score; the guide allows bonus consideration for exceptional features or educational and social value.
