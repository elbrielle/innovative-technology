Individual · Cover letter, resume, and interview

Career Prep

You will prepare an application for a future technology career.

You research a career.You write an application.You interview if you advance.

What you make or do

  • Write a one-page cover letter and a job-specific resume of no more than two pages.
  • Combine the documents into a PDF and prepare for a mock job interview.

This year’s challenge

Choose a Digital Technology career in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, IT Support and Services, Network Systems and Cybersecurity, Software Solutions, or Unmanned Vehicle Technology.

How the competition works

Before the event

Research an eligible career and imagine that you have completed the realistic education and training needed for an entry-level position. Submit the application PDF by the national pre-conference deadline.

First round

Judges evaluate your cover letter and resume to select semifinalists.

If you advance

Take part in a ten-minute mock interview. Bring an additional printed copy of your resume for the judges.

Know before you choose: The guide requires your real name but a fictional home address and telephone number. Do not add a headshot, cover page, or table of contents.

How your work is scored

Judges assess how clearly your application fits the job, its organization and writing, and your knowledge and communication in the interview.
