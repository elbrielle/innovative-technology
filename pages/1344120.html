Team of 2 · Twenty-four-hour design portfolio challenge

Technical Design

Your team develops and documents an engineering design in response to a new problem.

Interpreting the design briefComparing three drawn solutionsSubmitting a design portfolio

What you make or do

  • Create a portfolio PDF explaining the assigned problem and constraints and showing your brainstorming.
  • Include at least three hand-drawn solution sketches, each with a pros-and-cons list, an engineering drawing of your chosen design, and a written evaluation of that design.

This year’s challenge

There is no separate annual theme listed. The engineering problem is announced at the conference. The paper-airplane launcher brief in the guide is only an example.

How the competition works

Before the event

Practice brainstorming, sketching, engineering drawing, and assembling a PDF. You receive the actual design brief and submission directions at the conference.

First round

Receive the on-site design brief, work through the technical design process as a team, and submit the portfolio PDF within twenty-four hours.

How the event finishes

No separate semifinal round, presentation, or interview is listed. Judges evaluate the submitted design portfolio to determine the results.

Know before you choose: The portfolio needs three different hand-drawn solution options with pros and cons, followed by an engineering drawing and an evaluation of the selected design. All competition work must be completed by the two teammates.

How your work is scored

Judges score each portfolio component and the design process, including problem interpretation, brainstorming, the three alternatives, the final engineering drawing, and the evaluation.
