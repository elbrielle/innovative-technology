Team of 2 · On-site construction and performance testing

Problem Solving

Your team designs and builds a solution to a problem revealed at the conference.

Reading the on-site problemBuilding with issued materialsTesting the solution

What you make or do

  • Use the supplied challenge materials to design and construct a solution in ninety minutes.
  • Have the completed solution tested using the measurements announced for that problem. Some challenges require your team to be present for testing.

This year’s challenge

There is no separate annual theme listed. The problem, construction materials, and scoring measurements are provided at the conference.

How the competition works

Before the event

Prepare the required toolbox and safety eyewear, and practice solving unfamiliar construction problems. The competition solution must be made at the event.

First round

Receive the problem, evaluation criteria, and materials on site. Build within the ninety-minute limit, then submit the solution for testing.

How the event finishes

No separate semifinal round is listed. Judges use the test results to determine the final standings.

Know before you choose: Bring a toolbox that meets the size rules and wear approved safety eyewear. Only issued materials may become part of the solution, except the permitted glue and masking tape.

How your work is scored

The solution is scored using an objective measurement such as time, distance, or strength. The announced problem determines the measurement, and an earlier testing time breaks a tie.
