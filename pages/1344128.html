Team of 3–6 · Research display and portfolio

Biotechnology

You will explain how biotechnology can improve the food people grow or eat.

You research an issue.You build a display.You explain your findings if you advance.

What you make or do

  • Create a display explaining a biotechnology issue, its possible solutions, and its effects on people and the environment.
  • Prepare a printed research portfolio with an issue explanation, supporting evidence, references, work log, and any required photo consent forms.

This year’s challenge

The 2027 theme is Biotechnology in Food. Explain how biotechnology is used in food production and one way it could help farmers or consumers.

How the competition works

Before the event

Research the current theme and build your display and portfolio. Prepare three team representatives to explain the work if you advance.

First round

Bring the display and portfolio to the conference. Judges assess the displays first and then the portfolios of the highest-ranking entries.

If you advance

Three team members give a seven-minute presentation and answer questions for two minutes, with one minute for setup.

Know before you choose: This is a research-and-explanation event. The display cannot contain liquids, viruses, live plants, or animals.

How your work is scored

Judges assess your explanation, research, supporting evidence, display design, and portfolio. If you advance, they also assess your knowledge and presentation.
