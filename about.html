Smart Solutions curriculum

About this curriculum

Public course sequence and approved files for Smart Solutions.

Course content

Visual design, fabrication, coding, circuits, artificial intelligence, robotics, extended reality, communication projects, and a capstone. Modules include teacher guides, student directions, assignments, assessments, rubrics, and approved course files.

Authorship and sources

Elisha Lucero developed the course for Irving ISD. Lessons also use district materials, program resources, platform documentation, and credited third-party media. Attributions remain with the lesson or file where each source is used.

Navigation

Modules and items follow Canvas order.

Canvas access

Canvas is the course record for submissions and grades. District-only and restricted materials are excluded from the public site.
