Curriculum · CTE Innovative Technology

Designed & coded learning modules

A selection of units from my CTE Innovative Technology course. Each module is designed and built in Canvas using custom HTML and inline CSS, with embedded video, visual scaffolds, sentence stems, ESL supports, and clear deliverables. The goal of these modules is to be self-paced and to encourage student differentiation. Click any lesson to open the real thing. Curriculum may reference freely available resources from Verizon Innovative Learning HQ, MIT RAICA, and others as noted.
